# Mandatory Colors, Qula IMS

| Swatch | Hex | Name | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gold | `#E3BC51` | Gold Accent | Accent, highlight. Sparing use for emphasis, badges, key CTAs |
| Cream | `#F8EBD4` | Cream | Secondary surface. Subtle section backgrounds, cards |
| Off White | `#FFFCFC` | Off White | Primary background |
| Warm White | `#FFF8F4` | Warm White | Alternate background, hover surface |
| Charcoal | `#221F19` | Charcoal Ink | Primary text, dark foreground |

## Notes

- These five colors are the mandatory palette values. Names and roles above are for developer and design token clarity. The hex values themselves are fixed.
- Suggested usage: Off White and Warm White for backgrounds, Charcoal Ink for text, Cream for secondary surfaces and cards, Gold Accent used sparingly for emphasis such as active states, key actions, and highlights, not as a dominant color, consistent with the low-clutter, premium direction.
